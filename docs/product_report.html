A study companion for medical students. It answers exam-style and clinical-vignette questions from five standard textbooks and Eka Care's BODHI clinical graph, cites every claim, and declines when its sources don't cover the question.

01What the product does

Three kinds of questions, each with its own path through the system:

  • Factual and comparison questions (pharmacology, physiology, pathology, microbiology, medicine). Retrieval runs over 16,032 passages. Compound questions are split into sub-questions that are retrieved separately.
  • Clinical vignettes ("fever, headache and joint pain after a mosquito bite"). Symptoms are linked to BODHI nodes. The graph ranks candidate conditions and their specialists. The textbooks supply the treatment, and the graph's drug list cross-checks the answer.
  • Refusals. Personal dosing or treatment questions are declined before retrieval. Off-topic questions are declined when retrieval similarity falls below 0.60, or when the LLM finds the evidence insufficient.

04Clinical vignettes: what the graph proposes

Every vignette was "answered", but the quality of the differential varies. This review reflects my clinical judgement of the graph's top three conditions. A domain expert should confirm it.

Fever, headache and joint pain after a mosquito bite
Good
linked: joint pain, headache, fever → Chikungunya · Viral exanthem · Dengue
55-year-old man with crushing chest pain to the left arm and sweating
Good
linked: chest pain, sweating → Acute MI · Acute bronchitis · Angina (bronchitis is noise; "radiating to left arm" was not linked)
Excessive thirst, frequent urination and weight loss
Partial
linked: thirst, frequency → Gestational DM · Type 1 DM · DKA (wrong order; weight loss was not linked)
Child with fever, cough, runny nose and a rash spreading from the face
Weak
linked: fever, cough, rash → Viral exanthem · Viral disease · Dengue (measles missing; coryza and rash spread not linked)
25-year-old with 3 weeks of cough, night sweats and weight loss
Wrong
linked: night sweats, cough → Lung abscess · COVID-19 · Infective endocarditis (TB missing: BODHI's TB node lacks these symptoms)

The textbook half of each vignette answer stays grounded. The weak spot is the graph's ranking: conditions tie on symptom count, and BODHI is missing classic symptom links.

05What changed today

  • DeepSeek backend (LLM_BACKEND=deepseek). The key is read from .env or the host's secret store only; it is never in git or chat. Calls retry on rate limits and 5xx responses, and fail with a clear message on a bad key.
  • Quality jump from the 3B model. On the local model the baroreceptor answer had the physiology backwards and vignette answers cited no textbook passages. On DeepSeek both are correct and cited.
  • Graph route limited to vignettes. It needs two or more symptoms, or one symptom plus a case cue. "Drug of choice for absence seizures" no longer goes through the symptom graph.
  • Answer cleanup. Echoed passage headers and bare [3] [8] openers are stripped.
  • Resilience. The app shows a readable message, never a traceback, when the LLM is down. This came up when Ollama quit during a test run.
  • Hosting kit: a Dockerfile, a bundle script that compacts the index from 379 MB to 274 MB, and an optional APP_PASSWORD login gate.
  • Product evaluation: eval/product_eval.py with 36 questions.

06Risks and known issues

High

No online host yet. Options: a Cloudflare tunnel from the laptop (nothing uploaded, laptop must stay on), HF PRO for a private Space, or an Oracle Cloud VM.

High

Copyright. The index is derived from textbooks obtained without a licence. Any hosted copy must be private or password-gated, and the UI shows only 300-character excerpts. A public release still waits on institutional access.

Medium

Data leaves the machine. Every answer sends up to 9 textbook passages and the question to DeepSeek's API. That's fine for the demo, but the team should know it. The offline fallback is LLM_BACKEND=ollama (weaker answers).

Medium

Vignette differentials, as shown in section 04. Missing symptom links in BODHI, and tied rankings.

Medium

Drug cross-check noise. BODHI's drug links include non-drugs (for example "yellow fever virus" under viral exanthem), and a match can come from a sentence that says the drug is not covered.

Low

Evaluation depth. The question set is mine and the judge is self-graded. Hand-written gold sections from the domain expert are still missing (eval/questions.jsonl).

Low

Latency. Multi-hop answers take 8 s at the 95th percentile. That's acceptable live, but the spinner should stay.
