# Demo follow-up email (send 17-18 Nov, after the demo)

**To:** [professor's name and email]  **Subject:** MedEvolv Mentor: three small asks after today's demo

Dear Dr [name],

Thank you for seeing MedEvolv Mentor today. In one line: it answers medical students only from their standard textbooks,
cites the book, chapter and page for every claim, and says "the books don't settle this" when they don't.

What we have measured, both internal and not yet externally reviewed: on our 39-question behaviour set (factual,
comparison, off-topic and personal-advice questions) it answered, declined or refused correctly on all 39, and every
answer was supported by its citations. Separately, a clinician-style review of 18 hard patient vignettes found major
problems in 12: general drug advice mixed into patient-specific answers. So we are not offering it to students until
that is fixed and reviewed. To take it further responsibly we would value three things from you:

1. **A reviewer.** Could you, or a PG resident you trust, check 9 exam questions whose model answers we have not been able
   to verify? About 30 minutes; we will send them in one sheet.
2. **A small pilot.** Permission to offer it free to one batch (about 150 students) for 4-6 weeks before their next
   professional exam, with anonymised usage logs and a "wrong citation" button, and a short report back to you.
3. **An introduction** to the college librarian or e-library committee, so we can understand which textbooks the college
   already licenses digitally. We would like the tool to run only on content the college is entitled to use.

Any one of these would help. Thank you again for your time.

Regards,
